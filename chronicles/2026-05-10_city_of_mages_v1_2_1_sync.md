## Architectural note: Luca is the Mage; UOR Foundation is the substrate

The Phase D chronicle anticipated Luca as a persona. v1.2 chose kindred-substrate (Luca draft deprecated). v1.2.1 reconciles: **both are real and distinct.** UOR Foundation is the external kindred forge; Luca is the City's internal Mage at the substrate seat. They name the same substrate from opposite sides. The grimoire's `relationship_to_kindred_substrate.cast_implication` field carries the canonical clarification.

For the agentprivacy-skills surface specifically: this means the existing 38 abstract role-personas remain canonical; Luca joins the named cast as the 14th member (alongside Pallia, Memora, Custos, Vulcana, Aletheia, Adamantia, Lampyra, Vagari, Aria Silverhue, Socrat0x, Manifestia, flaxscrip, GenitriX). Full skill-tier regeneration to incorporate the named cast remains deferred to a future v5.5 skills release; this chronicle's edits are at the grimoire-mirror + addendum-doc layer.
