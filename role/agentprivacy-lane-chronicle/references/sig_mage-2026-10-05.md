# Worked example: the sig_mage chronicle on sig.golf (2026-10-05)

Board: Yukon `eigenlabs/sig-golf/full` (repo Layr-Labs/sig.golf): a Lean-certified hash signature scored S × C,
C = accepting verify cycle bound + ⌈W/256⌉. Lane: sig_mage, solver mitchuski.

## What the shared note contains

- Four levers, one figure each: **E8** (sign a lower tree's two root children instead of its root, −40 cycles),
  **C1** (an arithmetic polynomial MAC over the signing cache, −835 signing compressions spent on target steps,
  −16), **the witness trim** (1,280 bytes verify never touches, charge 95 → 90), **the credit filter** (the signer
  searches until ≥ 9 top digits are credited, −9, verifier image unchanged).
- Ten submissions (four promoted), a timeline, 90 citing PRs by 13 solvers (36 merged, coauthor in 20), where the
  record stands (jonasnick's WCT-9 v4 carries the trim and the credit floor), the proverb of *The Signature Hole*,
  and the City / harness passage.
- Files (in the lane's private working tree, `diagrams/`): `note_template.html` (the full page),
  `build_note.py` (fills figures, renders PDF + PNG), `export_figs.py` (figures for the Soul Sync letter),
  `citations_2026-10-05.json` (the snapshot), `sig_mage_chronicle.pdf` (the shared copy). Companions:
  `agentprivacy-docs/blog/the-signature-hole.md` (letter "Strokes in the Floor") and
  `cityofmages/tomes/proposed/the-signature-hole.md` (holes I–X).

## Corrections made between draft and shared copy (the rules in SKILL.md §3 came from these)

1. A figure stated the credit identity as `top layer = 1,086 − credit`; the proved form is
   `top cost + 69 + credit = 1,086`. Fixed in the figure and every reflection.
2. A combined score was mis-multiplied (41,167,192 written for 5,454 × 7,548 = 41,166,792).
3. "Every record since carries the trim" was checked against the twelve latest record claims (all W 22,984) before it
   stayed; "all three levers are in the current line" did not survive the next record (WCT-9 v4 keeps E8 only as its
   own variant) and was rewritten.
4. "Six more PRs carry the filter" became "name it": the count was a keyword match.
5. The footer cited local paths; replaced with the public record and the counting method.
6. "Four solvers ported C1" became "took C1 into their own submissions; none merged".
7. Pronouns for other solvers in the tome were he/his; changed to they/their.
8. The City of Mages was first described as a knowledge directory; the keeper's description is a verifiable trust
   community, with the tomes as early writings of how the agentprivacy work is getting there.
9. Layout: a forced page break left a half-empty page; the proverb and footer orphaned onto a last page; fixed with
   `break-inside:avoid` wrappers and a slightly tighter print font.
10. Render: the PDF was open in a viewer and silently not overwritten; the build now checks the file time.
11. The City of Mages section read as too forward for a board audience; reduced to a tag under the proverb, followed by a first-person "note from one solver" (one view of a shared course; the template is an open skill; room for more tomes) and the harness section.
