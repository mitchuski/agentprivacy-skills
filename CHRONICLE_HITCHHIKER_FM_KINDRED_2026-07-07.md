## What This Chronicle Records

A neighbouring Federated Wiki federation was recognised as **kindred** to the agentprivacy
corpus, and its studio's spec pages were catalogued as candidate/kindred skills. The
connection was not designed — it was **discovered**, in the way the UOR convergence was
discovered: two independently built corpora laid beside each other turned out to share a
vocabulary, a substrate, and a founding thesis.

**hitchhikers.earth** is a member-owned digital mutual society (founder Robbie Stamp / Wildly
Improbable Ltd, on the DNA of h2g2.com and 25 years of Field Research) building the Hitchhiker's
Guide to the Galaxy *for real*. **Hitchhiker FM** (`fm.ide.earth`, David Bovill) is its studio —
"a constitutional radio station, broadcasting the process through which a living constitution is
created." Both run on **Federated Wiki**, the same substrate as the agentprivacy farm.

The decisive fact: the bearer (Mitchell Travers) is **already named** in Hitchhiker FM's first
circle — **The Three Mice: David, Mitch, Max** — with a reserved spell-site at
`mitch.fm.ide.earth`. This is why the corpus's first *named, real* Oasis Protocol crossing
(Tome V Act 18) is made on this link and not another: the bearer already lives at the oasis.
